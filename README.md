# Wild Magic Items

A FoundryVTT module for D&D 5e: a large collection of homebrew magic items, many
built around roll tables that re-roll their effect each dawn, so the same item
behaves differently from one day to the next.

## Installation

Paste the following manifest URL into Foundry's module installer:

```
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hematic/wild-magic-items/main/module.json
```

## Contents

Six compendia, filed under a **Hematic's Magic Modules** compendium folder:

| Compendium | Type | Count | What's in it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wild Magic Items | Item | 12 | The headline items — unpredictable effects driven by roll tables |
| Awakened Items | Item | 42 | 14 items that grow through three tiers as they awaken |
| Magic Item Menagerie | Item | 42 | Wondrous items, potions, scrolls and tattoos |
| Wild Magic Tables | RollTable | 12 | The tables the items roll on (333 results total) |
| Wild Magic Actors | Actor | 32 | Creatures summoned by the items |
| Wild Magic Macros | Macro | 2 | Automation for items that need it |

### Wild Magic Items

Twelve items whose behaviour is rolled rather than fixed:

- **Alchemical Vial** — 30 possible brews
- **Crow Cloak** — the crows tell you one of 25 things
- **Deck of Many Creatures** — 19 cards, each summoning a creature
- **Drifting Cloak of Planes** — drifts to one of 24 planes daily
- **Fool's Scepter** — a different spell each day from a table of 60
- **Purse of the Mite** — 1d10 gp appears each dawn
- **Ring of Familiars** — a different familiar each day, from 30
- **Shifting Eye Patch** — 18 possible benefits
- **The Dreamer's Pillow** — 20 dreams
- **The Fickle Thicket** — 43 berry colours, each with its own effect
- **The Moult** — reconfigures into one of 20 forms
- **Warden's Shield** — protection at the cost of one of 14 daily curses

Eleven of the twelve pull from a matching table in **Wild Magic Tables**; the
Purse of the Mite uses a macro instead.

### Awakened Items

Fourteen items that advance through three tiers — base, awakened, and a final
form — gaining new abilities at each step:

Embersong · Oathplate · Shadowstitch · Stormcaller · The Archivist's Staff ·
The Ashen Mantle · The Hollow King · The Huntsman's Bow · The Pale Verdict ·
The Sentinel's Plate · The Shepherd's Crook · The Verdant Cloak · Voidpiercer ·
Wand of Unraveling

Rarity climbs with each tier, from uncommon up to legendary.

### Wild Magic Actors

Thirty real-world animals statted as NPCs — axolotl, capybara, fennec fox,
pangolin, quokka, serval and friends — one for each result on the Ring of
Familiars table, so the ring summons creatures bundled with the module rather
than anything you need to supply. Also included: a Goliath Werebear drawn by the
Deck of Many Creatures, and the planted form of The Undying Banner.

## Compatibility

- Foundry VTT **V13+** (verified on V14)
- **D&D 5e** system

### Optional

The **Deck of Many Creatures** table draws 10 of its 19 results from the
official **D&D Monster Manual** module. Without it those draws won't resolve;
the remaining results come from the 5e system's own SRD actors.
